# Art reference pack (provided by the project owner, 2026-11-23)

`STOCKHOLM_ASSETS/` is the owner's art-direction pack: an illustrated stylized-realism look (ink outlines, cel-shaded ramps), the founder **Doudou BA** of **OctOpus** (turnaround + detail sheets), Stockholm environment plates (Sergels torg, Stureplan, Norrmalm block), and specs for vehicles, buildings, interiors, props, shaders, cinematics and San Francisco.

The pack contains **2D concept images and written specs only, no 3D assets**. Its specs drive:
- the outline + cel-shading look in `WebPreview/` (and the planned UE5 `PP_GTA_Stylized` post-process and `M_GTA_Master` material),
- Doudou's model colours and proportions (hoodie `#9B87B8`, 182 cm),
- taxis and city buses in traffic, and Sergels torg's triangle paving, glass obelisk and glass-fronted cultural house.

**Trademark note:** `00_ART_DIRECTION/STK_ART_KEY_VISUAL_FOUNDER_GTA_001.png` carries the *Grand Theft Auto VI* logo (a Rockstar Games / Take-Two trademark) and an event brand on the cart. Use it as an internal style reference only; it can't ship in the game or marketing. The preview uses a crop that excludes both. The game's working title in the pack ("Founder City VI") should also be cleared before any public use.
