# Stockholm Modular Building Specs (UE5)
- **Grid Unit:** 400 cm x 400 cm modular wall panels, 350 cm floor-to-floor height.
- **Norrmalm / Sergels Torg:** Modernist concrete, anodized aluminum mullions, low-iron curtain glass, triangular Plattan paving (`#1E2024` diabase & `#EAE8E3` Ekeberg marble).
- **Stureplan / Östermalm:** 19th-century warm ochre/cream lime plaster facades, granite ground-floor plinths, standing-seam dark zinc & patinated copper mansard roofs.
